## toki open (Preamble)

nasin JFA (Janus Facing Architecture) li pana e tomo wan taso tawa kulupu ni: kipisi nasin (protocol tier) pi kipisi lawa (Governance layer) — kulupu pi mani ala, pi nasin 501(c)(3), pi awen e ilo kepeken nasin copyleft; lipu lawa ni li lawa e ona. nasin pi jan kulupu ona li lawa linja (orchestration) pi kipisi lawa pi nasin JFA. lon tenpo ni la, sinpin pi kipisi lawa li lon ilo Slack; kulupu Salesforce li pana e ona — ni li tomo pi tenpo lili, pi jo e nimi, tan nasin tomo pi §5.6 (P1-005). kulupu li pali e ilo sin tawa ni: ilo pi jan ante ni li wile ala.

## Article I — kulupu lawa

**1.1 nimi en sona.** nimi pi kulupu ni li Network Theory Applied Research Institute, Inc. ("NTARI" anu "kulupu NTARI"). ona li kulupu lawa pi mani ala. tomo lawa ona li lon tomo Luwiwi, lon ma Kentuki. nanpa mani ona (EIN) li 92-3047136.

**1.2 lawa pi ma.** kulupu NTARI li kama tan lawa pi ma Kentuki pi kulupu pi mani ala: KRS Chapter 273. ma Mewika li sona e ona sama kulupu pi mani ala, tan nasin 501(c)(3) pi lipu lawa mani (Internal Revenue Code). lipu open pi kulupu (articles of incorporation) li jo e toki ale pi wile pi lawa ni. lipu lawa ni li toki sin ala e toki ni.

**1.3 tomo sitelen en jan sitelen.** kulupu NTARI li jo e tomo sitelen e jan sitelen tan wile pi KRS Chapter 273. nimi ona pi tenpo ni li lon lipu pi jan lawa lipu pi ma Kentuki (Secretary of State). lipu ni li toki sin ala e ona.

**1.4 tan seme.** kulupu NTARI li lon tan ni: ona li awen e nasin JFA e ijo kulupu pi sewi ona:

(a) ona li jo e lipu suli pi nasin JFA, li awen e ona, li ante e ona; li awen e ilo lukin (conformance suite) pi ken pali;

(b) ona li awen e ilo JFA sama ijo kulupu pi jan ale, kepeken nasin copyleft;

(c) ona li alasa sona e nasin kulupu, e ilo pi toki kulupu, e nasin mani pi insa kulupu; li pana e lipu sona ni tawa jan ale; li

(d) ona li pana e sona sama tawa jan ale.

**1.5 lipu pi nimi awen.** lon lipu lawa ni la, "lipu suli" li lipu [janus-facing-architecture.md](https://github.com/NTARI-RAND/janus/blob/main/janus-facing-architecture.md); kulupu NTARI li awen e ona. "nasin 12" li kipisi pi lipu suli pi nimi "The Lines That Cannot Be Crossed". "ilo lukin" li ilo pi ken pali en lipu pi nasin awen; tu ni li lukin e lipu suli. "lipu pi nasin pi ike en lawa" li lipu [jfa-dispute-mechanics.md](https://github.com/NTARI-RAND/janus/blob/main/jfa-dispute-mechanics.md).

## Article II — nasin nanpa wan

nasin ni li lawa pali pi sona lipu. jan li lukin e toki ante ale pi lipu lawa ni la, ona o lukin kepeken ni: toki ale li pali tawa nasin ni.

**2.1 pali kulupu.** kulupu pi pali e nasin li kulupu sama pi lukin e nasin. pali tu ni li tawa lon insa pi jan kulupu ale lon tenpo ale. jan li kama ala kulupu tu: jan lawa en jan pi lawa ala li lon ala. lipu lawa ni li ken ala pali e kulupu insa pi ni: ona li wile taso, li kute ala.

**2.2 ken pi weka (nasin "gravity").** kipisi ale pi nasin — en kulupu insa ale pi kulupu NTARI — li lawa tan ni: weka tan ona li ike seme? weka li pona la, esun ante li lawa; lipu ni li pana ala e lupa lawa. weka li ike la, jan kulupu li pana e mu. weka li ike mute mute la, wile ale li awen ken kama ante. jan li nanpa e ike pi weka lon tenpo ni pi ilo anu kulupu, lon ala tenpo open ona: lukin pi pona lon tenpo pi mani awen li ken lili tawa tenpo pi mani pi kulupu. **wawa ale li wile e lukin.** toki pi lipu ni li pana e wawa la, ona o nimi e lukin pi wawa ni; lukin pi wawa li ken ala kama sona la, wawa li lon ala, tawa tenpo ni: jan kulupu li pana e lukin.

**2.3 pini ala pi wile kulupu.** wile pi jan kulupu li pini ala lon tenpo ale. jan kulupu ale li ken pana sin e ijo pini tawa kulupu, tan §6.7.

**2.4 sona pi jan li lon ala lipu pi jan ale.** lipu pi jan ale pi nasin li jo e nanpa (hash), e kule nasin (type), e tenpo, e nasin tawa sona taso. ona li jo ala e toki suli, li jo ala e nimi jan. pali lawa pi kulupu NTARI pi lon lukin li sama: lipu, mu, en lipu pali pi pana li toki e jan kepeken nasin tawa sona taso; sona jan li lon lipu tan wile pi pali ni taso.

**2.5 lipu li awen.** kulupu NTARI li weka ala e sona; ona li pana e toki sin lon poka. lipu pi kama kulupu, mu, sitelen kama, lipu pana, en wile pi lipu li kama pona kepeken toki sin taso. sona ala pi pali lawa li weka, li kama ala ante.

## Article III — jan pi kulupu

**3.1 nasin pi kama jan kulupu.** jan li kama jan pi kulupu NTARI kepeken nasin tu: ona li lawa e ilo JFA pi kulupu (federated instance), anu ona li jan pali jo (prosumer) lon ilo pi kulupu tan §3.8 — jan pali jo li kama lon kulupu pi toki awen anu kulupu pi lawa, tan wile ona tan §3.9. jan pi ilo linja (orchestrator) li lawa e ilo pi wan e jan pi ilo suli lon ma mute lon kulupu mute. jan pi ilo suli (operator) li lawa e ilo sinpin; jan pali jo (jan kepeken) li kepeken e ona. nasin pali li sewi anpa, taso lon lawa la, jan ilo pi kule tu ni li sama suli; jan pali jo li sama suli lon kulupu ona. lipu ni li toki e "jan ilo" (operating member) la, ni li jan kulupu tan ilo pi kulupu; "jan pali jo" li jan kulupu tan §3.8.

**3.2 ilo pi kulupu.** ilo li "pi kulupu" (federated) lon tenpo ni taso: ale ni li lon:

(a) ona li jo ala e pakala pali pi ni: §10.6 li toki e ijo pi jan kulupu tan pakala ni;

(b) ona li jo e jan lukin awen tu anu mute; ona li kama tan nasin alasa pi §10.2, li kama jo e mani tan §10.2, li jan ante tan nasin §3.10. jan lukin li lili tawa tu la, ilo o sitelen e nimi "kulupu ala" (unfederated) lon ona; ilo ni li ken ala kama jan kulupu;

(c) ona li pana e nanpa ona tawa lipu suli pi jan ale lon kipisi ilo ale;

(d) ona li pana e ilo ona, e ante ale ona, tawa jan ale, sama wile pi nasin copyleft; li

(e) pali ona li pakala ala e nasin 12.

**3.3 sitelen kama.** jan li kama jan kulupu lon tenpo ni: jan pi awen e lipu li sitelen e kama ona. sitelen kama li pali nasin taso; ona li tan wile lawa ala: jan pi awen e lipu li lukin e §3.2 tawa jan ilo, anu li lukin e esun pini pi wile §3.8 tawa jan pali jo — tan lipu suli pi jan ale en lipu pana, tan ala toki pi jan pi ilo suli. ona li sitelen e kama lon lipu pi kulupu lawa (§9.5), e kulupu pi wile pi jan pali jo tan §3.9. jan pi awen e lipu li wile ala sitelen la, ona o toki e ni: "wile seme li pakala?" — jan li ken utala e ni kepeken Article XII.

**3.4 jan wan li jo e mu wan.** jan kulupu ale li jo e mu wan lon kulupu ale ona; mu li kama ala tu, lon mute pi ilo ona lon kipisi wan. jan ilo li lon kulupu pi kipisi ale pi pali ona. jan pali jo li lon kulupu wan taso — kulupu pi toki awen anu kulupu pi lawa, tan wile ona tan §3.9 — li lon ala kulupu ante. jan wan pi kulupu li pana e wile pi kulupu tawa kulupu NTARI tan §5.3; ni la nanpa jan li lawa lon insa pi kulupu wan taso, li lawa ala lon poka. wawa lawa li sama mani kulupu: jan li ken ala esun e ona.

**3.5 jan ilo li jan lawa pi ilo ona.** jan ilo li jan anu kulupu pi lawa e pali pi ilo ona. kama jan kulupu ona li jo e pali wile pi Article X tawa esun ale pi ilo ona. jan pali jo li lawa ala e ilo, li jo ala e pali wile pi Article X.

**3.6 pini (lapse).** ilo pini pi jan kulupu li pakala e §3.2, li kama ala pona lon tenpo suno 90 la, ken pi jan kulupu li pini (lapse). pini li kama lipu kepeken toki sin, kepeken kule ona — "pini pali" anu "pini ken" (ceased anu lapsed); ona li weka ala e tenpo pini pi jan. jan pini li ken kama sin kepeken nasin sitelen sama pi jan sin — taso Article XII li lawa la, ona li lawa.

**3.7 pali tu li tawa lon tenpo ale.** jan kulupu ale li jan pi pali e nasin, li jan pi lukin e nasin, lon tenpo sama. kulupu li ken ala pali e kule jan kulupu pi pali wan taso. kama jan pali jo li kule ni ala: jan pali jo li pali e nasin kepeken esun ona, li lukin e nasin kepeken mu ona lon kulupu pi wile ona tan §3.9.

**3.8 kama jan kulupu pi jan pali jo.** jan pali jo pi ilo pi kulupu li jan pi kulupu NTARI, lon kulupu pi toki awen anu kulupu pi lawa — tan wile ona tan §3.9 — lon kulupu ni taso, lon tenpo pi sitelen kama tan §3.3. sitelen kama li wile e ni: esun pini pi jan ni li lon lipu suli pi jan ale, lon jan esun ante tu anu mute. jan li lukin e ni tan lipu suli pi jan ale taso, tan ala toki pi jan pi ilo suli. nanpa ni li tan jan wan, li tan ala nanpa ilo (account). kama jan kulupu ni li jo e mu pi §3.4; li jo e ken kama jan lawa pi §8.2 anu §8.3, tan kulupu pi wile ona; li jo ala e pali wile pi Article X. jan pali jo li pana e mu lon ijo ale pi wile pi kulupu ona — e ante pi lipu lawa ni tan §15.1 kin: **jan pi lawa anpa li jo e mu lon nasin pi lawa ona.** suli pi mu ona li lawa ala tan kule ijo; ona li lawa tan nasin pi jan wan pi §5.3: kulupu ale li pana e mu wan tan luka, lon suli ale pi nanpa jan ona. ken ni li pini lon tenpo ni: jan li wile e pini, anu esun pini ala pi jan ni li lon ilo pi kulupu, li kama ala sin lon tenpo suno 90; pini li kama lipu kepeken toki sin tan §3.6.

**3.9 wile pi kulupu.** lon tenpo pi sitelen kama tan §3.3 la, jan pali jo li wile e kulupu pi toki awen anu kulupu pi lawa; jan pi awen e lipu li sitelen e wile ni lon lipu pi kulupu lawa tan §9.5. wile ni li pana e tomo pi mu en pi ken kama jan lawa pi §8.2 anu §8.3 taso, li pana e ala ante: ken ale pi Article IV li lon kulupu ale, lon tomo toki ale, lon ala wile ni. ijo pi jan pali jo li kama lon tomo toki wan la, jan ni li ken toki lon tomo toki ni tan §4.1(d) — kulupu pi mu ona li ken ante.

jan pali jo li ken ante e kulupu ona lon sike wan, lon tenpo wan taso. tenpo ante li open lon tenpo 00:00 UTC pi tenpo suno 17 pi mun tu wan (March), li pini lon tenpo pi suno sama (March equinox) lon nasin tenpo UTC. jan pi awen e lipu li pana e tenpo pi suno sama pi sike kama, tan lipu nanpa pi mun en suno (ephemeris), lon tenpo pi suno sama pini anu lon sinpin ona. wile ante ale pi insa pi tenpo ni li kama lon lon tenpo pi suno sama: jan pi awen e lipu li sitelen e lipu jan sin tan §9.5. tenpo ante la, wile pini li awen. ante ni li pali pi jan taso: jan pi ilo suli anu jan pi ilo linja li ken ala pali e ona, li ken ala lawa e ona, li ken ala awen e ona, li ken ala wile e ijo tan ona — tan §4.5. jan li pini, li kama sin lon insa pi sike wan la, ona li jo e kulupu pini ona, tawa open pi tenpo ante kama.

tenpo pi suno sama li nasin open tan ni: ona li lon nasin tenpo ala pi kulupu wan. sike li open lon tenpo ante lon kulupu ante; taso ma li lon lon sama lon tawa suno, tawa kulupu ale.

**3.10 jan lukin li jan ante.** jan lukin li "ante" (independent) tawa ilo lon tenpo ni: ona li jan lawa ala pi ilo ni; ona en jan lawa pi ilo li lon ala lawa sama; ona li jo ala e ijo mani lon jan lawa pi ilo; ona li jan pali jo ala pi ilo ni. jan lukin li jo e ijo mani lon jan pi ijo lawa la, ona o toki e ni lon sinpin pi pali ona, o weka tan pali lon tenpo pi wile tu; toki ni li kama lipu tan §2.5. jan pi kipisi ilo li ken ala kama tan nasin alasa pi §10.2 anu §10.9 lon tenpo ni: nanpa mute (mode) pi pana pi lawa utala ona tan §10.4 li −1; nanpa mute li nasin wile pi ken pi §9.11, li lon ala lukin sama sona pi jan. jan lukin li toki e "mi ante" lon lipu, lon tenpo alasa; jan li ken utala e ni kepeken Article XII, sama wile ante ale.

## Article IV — ken pi jan pali jo

**4.1 ken pi wile ala e kama jan kulupu.** nasin JFA li pana e ken tawa jan pali jo ale. lipu lawa ni li wile ala e kama jan kulupu tawa ken ni. jan pali jo, lon ala kama jan kulupu, li ken:

(a) lukin e pana ale lon lipu suli pi jan ale, kepeken ijo en nanpa len (salt) pi lipu ona;

(b) open e utala (dispute) sama jan lipu pi esun; pana e nanpa lon lawa pi jan pi lawa utala — sama toki pi lipu pi nasin pi ike en lawa;

(c) toki sin e pini pi ken esun (trust suspension), anu utala e wile weka — kepeken nasin pi Article XII; li

(d) toki tawa tomo toki pona lon pali ale pi jo e ona.

**4.2 ken, en mu.** ken pi §4.1 li toki en nasin taso; ona li wile ala e kama jan kulupu. mu li lon ala ken ni: jan pali jo li kama jan kulupu tan §3.8 la, ona li jo e mu sama jan kulupu, tan nasin §3.4, lon kulupu pi wile ona tan §3.9, li pana e mu lon ijo ale pi wile pi kulupu ni — lon kulupu pi toki awen la, kama en weka pi jan lawa nanpa wan tan §8.2 kin; lon kulupu pi lawa la, kama en weka pi jan lawa nanpa tu tan §8.3 en wile weka tan §12.3 kin. wile pi kulupu li pana e tomo pi mu taso, li pana e ala ante; ken pi §4.1 li lon ma ale, lon ala wile ni.

**4.3 kulupu NTARI li pali tawa ken ni.** jan lawa ale en kulupu insa ale pi kulupu NTARI o kama jo e lipu pi jan pali jo, o sitelen e ona, o pali e ona — sama lipu pi jan kulupu, lon nasin sama.

**4.4 nasin tawa kama jan kulupu.** jan pi ilo suli ale o pana e ilo ni lon sinpin pi ilo ona: jan pali jo li ken kama jan kulupu tan §3.8, li ken wile e kulupu ona li ken ante e ona tan §3.9, li ken open e ijo li ken pana e mu lon tomo toki pi kulupu ona — sama ni: jan ilo li kama lon kulupu kepeken ilo lawa pi §5.3. weka pi wan pi ilo ni li pakala pali tan §10.6.

**4.5 mu pi jan pali jo li mu ona taso.** jan pi ilo suli anu jan pi ilo linja li ken ala pana e mu pi jan pali jo, li ken ala lawa e ona, li ken ala awen e ona, li ken ala wan e ona. ona li ken ala toki e ni: "nasin mu sina li lawa e pali, e mani kulupu, e ken sina." — li ken ala lawa e ijo tan kulupu pi wile pi jan tan §3.9. mu ale pi jan pali jo pi ilo wan li lon lipu, sama pana ona: kulupu mu pi tawa sama li lon lukin lon lipu. pali ante li pakala pali tan §10.6, li tan pi wile weka tan §12.3.

## Article V — tomo toki pi kulupu

**5.1 tomo toki.** kulupu NTARI li jo e tomo toki wan tawa kipisi ale pi nasin: ilo (Substrate), sona (Record), toki awen (Covenant), lawa (Governance), esun en sona (Economy & Information). jan kulupu ale li lon tomo toki pi kipisi ale pi pali ona. tomo toki li tomo ni: jan pi kipisi wan li pona e pakala, li awen e ken awen lon nasin sama, li ante e nasin kulupu, li pali e lawa pi kipisi ona. tomo toki pi toki awen en tomo toki pi lawa li jo kin e jan pali jo pi wile e ona tan §3.9.

**5.2 tomo toki pona.** lipu ni li toki e "tomo toki pona" la, ni li tomo toki pi kipisi pi kama ijo. ijo li lon kipisi mute la, tomo toki pi lawa li pona.

**5.3 kulupu.** kulupu (federation) li kulupu jan pi jan kulupu pi kipisi wan (ilo, sona, toki awen, lawa, esun en sona) — jan pi ilo suli en jan pi ilo linja ona; lon kipisi pi toki awen en kipisi lawa la, jan pali jo pi wile e ona tan §3.9 kin. jan ilo li kama lon kulupu lon tenpo sama kepeken ilo lawa (console) lon monsi pi ilo JFA; kulupu NTARI li pali e ilo ni. jan pali jo li kama kepeken ilo pi §4.4. kulupu ale li wile e jan wan (delegate); jan wan ni li jan lawa pi kulupu ni tan §7.2. jan wan li pana e mu wan pi kulupu lon ijo ale pi lipu ni pi "jan wan mute". kulupu li ken weka e jan wan ona lon tenpo ale, tan wile ala, lon tenpo sama.

**5.4 pali pi jan lawa.** tomo lawa ale pi kulupu NTARI li jo e jan wan pi kulupu wan. kulupu li nasin ni: jan kulupu li lukin e tomo lawa ni lon tenpo ale: ona li lukin e pali pi tomo lawa; jan wan ona li pali tawa tomo lawa lon ijo pi lipu ni, sama §12.5(d).

**5.5 lipu pi kulupu.** jan pi ilo linja en jan pi ilo suli li awen e lipu pi pali ona; lipu li awen, li kama ala weka, tan §2.5; ona li awen e nasin §2.4.

**5.6 tomo kulupu (venues).** tomo kulupu pi kulupu NTARI — ilo pi tomo toki, pi kulupu, pi kama kulupu — o ken weka: lipu lawa ale o ken tawa ale; tomo wan li ken ala kama ilo pi ni: weka ona li pini e pali pi kulupu NTARI. ilo pi jan ante (proprietary) li ken lon tenpo lili taso, kepeken nimi, kepeken nasin weka pi lipu lon. kulupu pi jan lawa li wile e tomo lon kepeken lipu nasin (policy): lipu ni li nimi e tomo, li toki e tenpo lili ona, li sitelen e nasin weka. ante pi tomo li pali nasin taso; ona li wile ala e ante pi lipu lawa ni.

**5.7 nasin mu pi jan wan.** jan wan li pana e mu pi kulupu ona sama wile pi kulupu, li pana e ala ante. jan wan li pana ante tawa wile kulupu, anu li pana lon tenpo pi wile ala kulupu la, kulupu li ken weka e ona tan §5.3; lipu pi kulupu lawa li sitelen e wile pi kulupu lon poka pi mu pana: jan ale li ken lukin e tu ni. kulupu li kama ala wile lon insa pi tenpo pi §6.4 la, jan wan li pana ala e mu; lipu li sitelen e pana ala ni.

## Article VI — kama kulupu en wile

**6.1 nasin tu pi pali.** jan kulupu li pali lon kama kulupu pi tenpo sama (synchronous), anu kepeken mu pi tenpo ante (asynchronous). nasin tu li sama wawa. kulupu li pini ala lon insa pi tu: kulupu NTARI li awen toki lon tenpo ale tan §6.3.

**6.2 kama kulupu pi sike wan.** jan kulupu li kama kulupu lon sike ale, lon tenpo wan anu mute: ona li kute e lipu pi jan lawa en pi kulupu pi jan lawa, li pali e ijo pi kulupu NTARI. jan lawa li kama tan kulupu ona tan §6.8, li kama ala tan kama kulupu ni.

**6.3 toki pi tenpo ale.** kulupu ale en jan wan ale li awen toki lon tenpo ale, lon tomo kulupu lawa pi kulupu NTARI. ijo ala li wile e kama kulupu sin; kama kulupu ala li nasa: jan kulupu ale li ken pana e ijo tawa kulupu ona lon tenpo ale; ijo li open tawa wile kepeken toki sona pi §6.4, li kama wile tan §6.5. mu li lon tenpo ante (asynchronous): ijo ale li jo e tenpo mu ona; jan li pana e mu ona lon insa pi tenpo ni. jan li ken ala pana e mu ona tawa jan ante; jan wan li pana e wile pi kulupu tan §5.7; ona li pana ala e mu pi jan wan.

**6.4 toki sona en tenpo.** toki sona pi kama kulupu anu mu li toki e ijo pi wile kama, li kama lon tomo toki pona lon tenpo esun wan anu mute lon sinpin pi pini mu. tenpo mu pi nasin open pi mu pi tenpo ante li tenpo esun wan.

**6.5 nanpa pi lon en wile.** ijo pi kulupu NTARI li kama wile tan jan wan mute: kulupu ale li pana e mu wan kepeken jan wan ona tan §5.3. mu pi jan wan mute li lon lon tenpo ni: kulupu tu wan anu mute li pana. lipu ni li wile ala e nanpa suli ante la, nanpa suli pi mu pana li wile e ijo.

lon insa pi kulupu la, nanpa suli pi mu pana pi jan kulupu li wile e ijo. mu pi kulupu li lon lon tenpo ni: toki sona pi §6.4 li lon, li jan wan anu mute li pana. kulupu li jo e jan pali jo la, ijo li kama wile lon tenpo ni taso: nanpa suli pi mu pana li wile e ona, LI nanpa suli pi ilo pi jan mu li wile e ona — wile pi ilo wan li nanpa suli pi mu pi jan pali jo ona taso. jan pali jo li nanpa lon ilo pi kama ona tan ilo pi §4.4. kulupu pi jan lili li pakala ala pi wile pona kepeken nanpa suli sin: lipu pi mu ale li toki e nanpa jan pi kulupu lon tenpo ni — kepeken ilo, lon tenpo pi mu pi jan pali jo — ni la wile pi jan lili li lon lukin, li lon ala len.

lipu ni li pana e ijo tawa tomo toki anu kulupu, tawa ala kulupu NTARI — sama wile weka tan §12.3 — la, kulupu ni li wile e ona kepeken nanpa suli insa sama; mu pi jan wan mute li lon ala. lipu wile pi §12.5 li kama wile sama toki pi §12.5(d).

**6.6 pali lon lukin ale.** kama kulupu en mu li lon lukin pi jan pali jo en jan ale, kepeken nasin §2.4.

**6.7 open sin pi ijo pini.** jan kulupu ale li ken pana sin e ijo pini tawa kulupu, kepeken lipu lon tomo toki pona; lipu li toki e ni: wile pini seme li utala? pini seme li wile? ijo li kama lon mu kama. kulupu li awen e wile pini, li ante ala la, jan sama li ken ala open sin e ijo sama lon tenpo suno 90 — taso lipu ona li toki e ike lipu pi lon ala lon tenpo pi wile pini la, ona li ken; jan ante ale li ken lon tenpo ale.

**6.8 kama en weka pi jan lawa.** jan lawa ale li kama tan kulupu pi tomo lawa ona tan §7.2, li weka tan ona, kepeken nanpa suli pi mu pana lon kulupu ni tan §6.5 — jan lawa nanpa wan li tan kulupu pi toki awen; jan lawa nanpa tu li tan kulupu pi lawa; lon kulupu tu ni la, jan pali jo pi wile e ona li pana e mu tan §3.4. weka li wile ala e tan; ona li lon lon tenpo sama.

**6.9 nasin toki pi ijo suli.** kulupu NTARI li jo e nasin toki tawa ijo suli. nasin ni li tan lipu nasin (policy), li lon poka pi tomo kulupu lawa, li lon ala lipu lawa ni: ni la jan li ken ante e ona lon tenpo pi ante tomo. nasin lon ale o pali e lawa pi pana pi toki awen tawa esun: ike wan pi lipu lon li ken open sin e wan toki (synthesis); ike li kama ala nanpa insa (average). nasin pi nanpa insa e ike, anu nasin pi pini e wan toki lon sewi pi ike lipu, li lon ala lon suli pi pakala ni.

## Article VII — kulupu pi jan lawa

**7.1 jan wan, jan sewi ala.** jan lawa li jan wan pi kulupu pi wile e ona; kulupu ni li ken weka e ona. kulupu pi jan lawa (Board) li pali e ijo pi kulupu NTARI lon insa pi kama kulupu; ona li jo ala e wawa pi Article XV pi jan kulupu taso.

**7.2 jan en tenpo lawa.** tan pini pi tenpo open tan §16.2 la, kulupu pi jan lawa li jo e jan lawa luka; kulupu pi tomo lawa ale li wile e jan ona: jan lawa nanpa wan li tan kulupu pi toki awen; jan lawa nanpa tu li tan kulupu pi lawa; jan pi awen e lipu li tan kulupu pi sona; jan pi lawa e tomo pali li tan kulupu pi ilo; jan pi awen e mani li tan kulupu pi esun en sona. kulupu li wile lon tenpo ale — lon pini pi tenpo lawa, anu lon tenpo wile — tawa tenpo lawa pi sike wan; jan lawa li awen pali tawa kama pi jan kama. tenpo open li pini ala la, kulupu pi jan open pi §16.1 li kulupu pi jan lawa. nanpa pi tenpo lawa li jo ala e sewi; weka pi §6.8 li lawa e ona.

**7.3 tomo lawa pi jan ala.** tomo lawa li jan ala la, kulupu pi tomo ni li wile e jan sin, tan §6.8. kulupu pi tomo lawa pi jan ala li pali ala e lawa ante lon tenpo pi wile ala ona; ilo lawa li awen e ni.

**7.4 pali.** kulupu pi jan lawa li pali lon kama kulupu pi tenpo sama anu tenpo ante, kepeken toki sona pi tenpo esun wan tawa jan ona. nanpa suli pi jan li lon la, kama li lon; nanpa suli pi jan lon li wile e ijo. pali ona li lon lukin pi jan kulupu; lipu ona li awen tan §2.5. kulupu pi jan lawa li jo ala e wile pi kama kulupu pi tenpo awen: ona li ken pali lon tenpo ale kepeken lipu wile (written consent) tan KRS 273.375; lipu ni li awen tan §2.5 sama pali ale.

**7.5 wawa awen.** kulupu pi jan lawa li ken ala ante e lipu lawa ni, li ken ala ante e lipu suli, li ken ala weka e jan, li ken ala pana e awen pi lipu suli anu ilo lukin tawa jan ante, li ken ala pini e kulupu NTARI. wawa ni li pi jan kulupu taso. lipu ni li pana e ijo tawa jan wan mute la, jan lawa pi pana e wile kulupu tan §5.7 li pali sama jan wan pi kulupu ona, li pali ala sama kulupu pi jan lawa; kipisi ni li toki e ni: kulupu pi jan lawa li ken ala wile e ijo ni tan wile ona taso.

**7.6 lupa sin ala.** kulupu pi jan lawa li ken ala kama lupa wile (approval step) lon nasin pi lipu ni anu lipu suli pi jo ala e ona — sama sitelen kama pi §3.3, sama ante pi tenpo nasin tan Article XI.

## Article VIII — jan lawa

**8.1 tomo lawa.** jan lawa pi kulupu NTARI li jan lawa nanpa wan (President), li jan lawa nanpa tu (Vice President), li jan pi lawa e tomo pali (Workspace Administrator), li jan pi awen e lipu (Secretary), li jan pi awen e mani (Treasurer). kulupu pi tomo lawa tan §7.2 li wile e jan ale lon tenpo ale — lon pini pi tenpo lawa anu lon tenpo wile — tawa tenpo lawa pi sike wan; kulupu li ken weka e ona tan §6.8.

**8.2 jan lawa nanpa wan.** kulupu pi toki awen li wile e ona. jan lawa nanpa wan li lawa e alasa sona en pali sin (R&D) pi nasin pi kipisi pi toki awen; li lawa e ilo LBTAS API pi pana e nanpa toki awen pona tawa kulupu ilo ale; li alasa sona e ni: toki awen li ante e seme lon kulupu esun en jan kepeken ona — kepeken kin alasa sona pi wile pi kulupu pi toki awen; li lawa e pana nanpa pi toki awen pi §12.5; pana ni li pana e sona tawa alasa ni. jan ale pi kulupu ni li ken wile e tomo lawa ni — jan pali jo pi §3.8 kin.

**8.3 jan lawa nanpa tu.** kulupu pi lawa li wile e ona. jan lawa nanpa tu li lawa e sinpin pi kipisi lawa; ona en jan pi lawa e tomo pali (§8.6) li lawa e ilo lawa lon poka sama; ona li awen e lipu lawa ni kepeken wawa pi Article XII. jan ale pi kulupu ni li ken wile e tomo lawa ni — jan pali jo pi §3.8 kin.

**8.4 jan pi awen e lipu.** kulupu pi sona li wile e ona. jan pi awen e lipu li awen e lipu lawa, li pali e sitelen kama pi §3.3, li awen e lipu pi kulupu lawa pi §9.5, li kama jo li sitelen e lipu pana pi Article XI, li pana e toki sona ale pi wile pi lipu ni.

**8.5 jan pi awen e mani.** kulupu pi esun en sona li wile e ona. jan pi awen e mani li awen e lipu mani pi kulupu NTARI, li toki tawa kama kulupu pi sike wan, li pali sama wile pi lawa pi §1.2.

**8.6 jan pi lawa e tomo pali.** kulupu pi ilo li wile e ona. jan pi lawa e tomo pali li lawa e alasa sona en pali sin (R&D) pi nasin pi kipisi ilo en lawa linja pi kipisi ilo, li pana e pali lon insa pi ilo lawa, li lawa e ilo ni lon poka pi jan lawa nanpa tu.

## Article IX — awen pi nasin

**9.1 kulupu NTARI li awen e seme.** kulupu NTARI li awen e lipu suli e kulupu ilo pi nasin JFA, e ilo lukin (conformance suite) e lipu pi nasin awen ona, e lipu pi nasin weka anu awen (concept triage), e lipu pi wile sona (open questions).

**9.2 ante pi lipu suli.** lipu suli li kama ante tan nanpa suli pi mu pi jan wan mute taso; kulupu ale li wile lon insa tan §6.5. ante li kama lon lon tenpo ni taso: ilo lukin li pona lon lipu sin; tawa ni la, ilo li pona lon tenpo ni: nasin awen ale pi kule "lipu" (§9.14(a)) li pona; nasin awen pana, pi nimi "wan ala" tan §9.15, li pini ala e kama. nasin pi weka tan lipu pi nasin weka anu awen li awen weka — taso pali sama pi kama sin li ante e lipu ni, li toki e ni.

**9.3 wile pi lipu pi wile sona.** kulupu NTARI li awen e lipu pi wile sona lon tenpo ni. wile sona li kama lon lawa, li kama ala pona la, ona li kama lon lipu ni. lipu pi wile sona li majuna la, kulupu li toki ala e lon ona; jan kulupu ale li ken pana e majuna ni sama ijo tawa tomo toki pi lawa.

**9.4 anpa pi pana ken.** ilo JFA li jo e nasin pana GNU Affero General Public License, nanpa 3 anu nanpa sin ale (AGPL-3.0-or-later); lipu nasin li jo e nasin Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0). kulupu NTARI li ante ala e tu ni tawa nasin pi jan wan taso (proprietary), li kama jo e pana sin kepeken nasin sama anpa ni taso, li sitelen ala e lipu wile pi pana e ken ni: jan — kulupu NTARI kin — li pini e ijo open.

**9.5 lipu pi kulupu lawa.** jan pi awen e lipu li awen e lipu pi kulupu lawa: lipu ni li lon lukin ale, li awen, li kama ala weka. ona li sitelen e ni: sitelen kama en pini pi jan kulupu, en wile kulupu en ante ona tan §3.9; lipu pana pi Article XI; weka, kama sin, en kama sin pi jan weka tan Article XII; ante ale pi lipu suli en pi lipu lawa ni. lipu ni li awen e nasin §2.4.

**9.6 toki pona en nimi.** kulupu NTARI li sona e toki "mi pona tawa nasin" lon tenpo ni taso: ilo lukin pi ken pali li toki e nanpa nimi (ID) pi lipu pi nasin awen; toki ante ale la, kulupu li nimi e ona "toki pi jan sama taso" (self-attested). ilo pi pakala e wan pi nasin 12 li JFA lili ala: kulupu NTARI li sona ala e ona, li kulupu ala e ona, li nanpa ala e pali ona tawa kama jan kulupu.

**9.7 mani pi kulupu NTARI li mani ala pi kulupu ilo.** mani pi kulupu NTARI li mani nasin, lon mani ma pi poki mani ona. kulupu NTARI li pali ala e mani, li jo ala e mani kulupu pi kulupu ante, li lon ala insa pi esun ale pi nasin: ona li jan esun ala, li jan pi awen mani ala, li jan pi pini esun ala.

**9.8 toki awen.** kipisi pi toki awen pi nasin li **LBTAS** (Leveson-Based Trade Assessment Scale). lipu ni li toki e "toki awen", e "nanpa toki awen", e "pana toki awen" la, ni li LBTAS sama toki pi lipu suli. kulupu NTARI li awen e LBTAS tan §9.1, li pana e ken ona tan §9.4, li ante e ona tan §9.2 taso.

**9.9 nasin nanpa.** LBTAS li toki awen pi "mi ike ala e sina"; ona li nanpa esun ala. ona li kama tan nasin pi awen pona (safety): ike li ijo wan pi kama lon lukin; ona li kama ala nanpa insa pi kama len. nasin nanpa ona li jo e nasin 6 pi jo e sona, tan −1 tawa +4; nanpa ale li jo e sona awen ona, li lon ala linja nanpa pi ken ante. nanpa anpa, −1, li toki e ni: jan li kama jo e ike, anu jan ante li kepeken ike e ona, anu jan li pali tawa ona lon lawa ala anu kepeken wile ike.

**9.10 nasin wawa pi toki awen.** ilo li pona tawa toki awen lon tenpo ni taso: ale ni li lon:

(a) **sona pi jan li lon ala nanpa wan.** ilo li pana ala e sona pi jan (reputation). ona li pana e lipu: nanpa pi pana lon nasin 6 ale, en nanpa ale, lon lipu LBTAS pi §9.11. sona pi jan li ni: jan pali jo li lukin e lipu ni, li pali e sona ona kepeken lawa ona — lukin ona taso pi tenpo pini pi jan ante; ilo li nanpa ala e ona, li toki ala e ona, li linja ala e ona. nanpa insa pi ilo ale, nanpa insa pi kule wan, nanpa ale wan li lon ala — lon pana ilo ala, lon lipu ala, lon lukin ala.

(b) **−1 li pakala; ona li jo e tan.** kule pi palisa −1 lon lipu LBTAS li loje: kule en sitelen anpa (−) li toki e kule ike. ike li kama lon lukin, li kama ala lili; esun ale pi kama jo e −1 o jo e nimi. −1 ale li jo e toki tan pi nimi 500 anu lili; toki li wile lon tenpo sitelen pi pana. −1 pi toki ala li weka; toki pi suli mute li weka, li kama ala lili kepeken len. nasin 0 tawa +4 li wile ala e toki. tan §2.4 la, nanpa (hash) pi toki taso li tawa lipu pi jan ale; toki li awen lon poki pi jan pana, li ken weka; jan li lukin la, ilo li lukin e toki lon nanpa ni.

(c) **pana li tawa nasin tu, li sama.** jan tu pi esun pini li pana e nanpa tawa jan ante; toki ale li ken kama jo e toki ante; weka pi toki li toki sin pi lon lukin, li weka lipu ala. jan wan li lawa e jan ante anu li pali tawa ona la, jan ni li jo e nasin toki ante.

(d) **pana li jo e kule nasin (relation).** pana ale li jo e kule pi nasin pana ona: jan lukin li ken ala wan e nasin tu. esun, lawa pi utala, en pona pi wile lawa li kule pi ilo esun; toki sona en nasin sona li kule pi ilo sona. ilo pi ma ante li nimi e kule ona, li lawa tan nasin sama. pana pi §12.5 li jo e kule lawa; jan lawa nanpa wan li nimi e ona. wan pi kule mute li nanpa insa weka pi (a), lon kule, lon ala pana.

(e) **sona pi jan li toki e "ken anu ken ala"; ona li toki ala e "mute seme".** sona pi jan li wile e ni taso: jan pali jo li ken esun kepeken ken awen, anu ken ala. nanpa pi suli esun li tan esun en tan nanpa wan pi kulupu ale pi §10.2; nanpa ni li kama ala tan sona pi jan. wan pi tu ni li pali sin e nanpa mani jan (credit score); ni li pakala pi kipisi ni.

(f) **sona pi jan li lon ilo wan taso; ona li tawa ala, tan nasin open.** nimi jan li nanpa len (hash) pi ilo wan; nimi pi wile jan li weka. tawa pi sona jan tawa ilo ante li wile pi jan kulupu taso; ona li nasin open ala pi ilo.

**9.11 lipu LBTAS.** lipu LBTAS li nasin lukin pi sona jan pi toki awen. lon kule ale la, nasin 6 — −1 tawa +4 — li nimi pi palisa ale; anpa pi palisa ale la, nanpa pi pana ale pi kama tawa jan ni lon nasin ni li lon. palisa −1 li jo e kule loje: kule ike li lon lukin tan kule en tan sitelen anpa. lipu li pana e nanpa mute e nanpa ale; ona li pana e nanpa wan ala. palisa li kama ala wan e palisa ante; kule li kama ala wan e kule ante; nanpa wan pi toki e lipu ale li lon ala lipu, li lon ala poka ona. lipu lawa ni li pana e wile tawa pana nanpa, li pana ala e nasin ante la — sama §12.3(b) tawa pini lon ilo wan — nanpa mute (mode) pi pana li nasin wile; ona li lon ala lukin sama sona pi jan.

**9.12 lukin li ken suli.** pana e nanpa, en lukin e lipu pana pi tenpo pini, li ken tu ante; ken tu li kama tan nasin tu ante; ken li pakala la, ona li pini (fails closed).

**9.13 ilo lukin.** ilo lukin li ilo pali pi ni: lipu suli li kama lukin lon lipu suli sama. ona li jo e ilo pi ken pali en lipu pi nasin awen pi jo ona. lipu pi nasin awen li pana e wawa tawa lipu suli: nasin awen pi lon ala lipu ni li lawa e ala; toki pona pi toki ala e nanpa nimi pi lipu ni li "toki pi jan sama taso" tan §9.6.

**9.14 lipu pi nasin awen en nasin pali ona.** nasin awen ale pi lipu li jo e nanpa nimi awen (ID), e kipisi pi lipu suli pi wile jo e ona, e nasin pali pi toki e ni: ilo pi awen e ona li lon seme:

(a) **lipu.** ilo lukin li lukin e ona ale, lon toki pi lipu suli.

(b) **ilo pali.** ilo lukin lili pi lon poka pi ilo pali taso li ken lukin e ona. nasin li pini lon tenpo ni: poki ilo li jo e ilo lukin pi toki e nanpa nimi.

(c) **lipu lawa.** lipu lawa wan en ilo tomo ona taso li ken lukin e ona. nasin li pini lon tenpo ni: lipu ni en ilo ona li toki e nanpa nimi.

**9.15 nasin awen pana li jo e nimi "wan ala".** ilo lukin li toki ala e "mi lukin e ni" tawa nasin awen pi pali ala ona. nasin awen pi kule "ilo pali" anu "lipu lawa" li jo e nimi "pana, wan ala" (delegated, unbound) tawa tenpo ni: ijo li toki e nanpa nimi ona. kulupu NTARI li nanpa e ona sama "pona ala" tawa wile ale pi lipu ni pi toki pona — taso tawa kama pi §9.2 ala. nanpa pi nasin awen wan en wan ala li kama lipu lon pali ale pi ilo. toki pi "nasin awen pana li pona" li toki pi jan sama, lon len pi ilo lukin; kulupu NTARI li sona ala e ona.

**9.16 ante pi lipu pi nasin awen.** ante pi lipu ni — pana pi nasin awen sin, weka pi wan, ante pi nanpa nimi anu kipisi anu nasin pali — li ante pi lipu suli, li kama tan §9.2. nanpa nimi pana li kama ala sin tawa ijo ante; nanpa nimi weka li awen weka. nasin awen sin li kama tan nasin awen weka la, lipu li sitelen e linja mama ni.

**9.17 awen pi ilo lukin.** pona pi ilo lukin pi ante ala e nanpa nimi ale, e kipisi ale, e nasin pali ale, li pali awen taso: ona li kama lon lukin ale lon tomo toki pi kulupu lawa, li wile ala e mu. jan kulupu ale li ken pana e pona; jan ale li ken pali e ilo lukin: ona li lon lukin ale, li ken pali tan jan ale tan §9.4; pali pi ken sin ala tan jan pi kulupu NTARI ala li pali ala.

**9.18 ilo lukin pakala li ijo kulupu.** ilo lukin li pakala lon lipu suli pi tenpo ni la, pakala li kama lon lipu pi wile sona tan §9.3, li awen ijo lon tomo toki pi kulupu lawa, tawa tenpo pini ona: pona pi ilo anu ante pi lipu. pakala li awen la, kulupu NTARI li sona ala e toki pona, e wile pi jan kulupu, e lipu pana — tan nasin awen pi jo e pakala ni.

**9.19 awen poki pi lipu suli pi jan ale.** kulupu NTARI li pana e mani — tan mani nasin ona tan §9.7 — tawa awen poki lon kipisi ilo, tawa pana pi lipu suli pi jan ale pi esun ale pi tawa lon jan pi ilo linja: lipu pi wan e kulupu ale li kama jo e mani tan kulupu ala wan. mani ni li esun e poki tan esun pi kipisi ilo, li jo ala e mani kulupu lon tenpo ale. ona li pali ala e tomo wan, e poki wan, e jan esun wan pi weka tan §13.1: lipu sama li lon poki pi jan ale pi esun ale; jan ale li ken pana e mani tawa awen pi lipu sama. ona li tawa jan awen kama tan §13.3. jan pi awen e mani li toki e mani ni lon lipu mani pi §8.5.

## Article X — pali wile pi jan kulupu

**10.1 pali pi jan pi ilo linja taso.**

**lawa esun.** o pana e tawa esun pi ilo mute e pali pi nanpa toki awen tawa jan pi ilo suli, tan nasin — lon ala lawa pi utala;

**pali nasin.** o awen e pali awen pi wan e pona (CI/CD) pi pona e lawa, pi awen e nasin;

**pali pi toki awen.** o pana e nanpa toki awen pi nasin tu lon ma ale pi ni: jan pi ilo linja en jan pi ilo suli li pana e sona anu ijo — tan §9.10.

**10.2 pali pi jan pi ilo suli taso.**

**lawa nasin.** o pana e esun pi lawa pi toki awen tawa jan pali jo (jan kepeken), tan nasin;

**wan linja.** sina ken la, o wan e kulupu pi jan pali jo kepeken pana tawa jan pi ilo linja;

**pali nasin.** o awen e CI/CD pi pona e lawa, pi awen e nasin;

**pali pi toki awen.** o pana e nanpa toki awen pi nasin tu lon ma ale pi pana sona anu ijo pi jan pi ilo linja en jan pi ilo suli, tan §9.10; o lawa e pakala pi toki awen lon insa pi jan pali jo ona; o pali e pona ale pi kama tan lawa ni.

**pakala mani.** o sitelen e pakala mani (default) lon lipu kepeken toki sin, kepeken kule ona — "moli", "weka", "lawa", anu "sona ala" — o weka ala e ona; o pana e nanpa pakala pi tenpo poka pi ilo: mani moli pi kama lon tenpo sike poka, lon nanpa pi esun ale.

**nanpa mani.** sina kepeken mani pi kulupu la, o pana e nanpa wan pi kulupu ale: nanpa wan sama tawa jan ale; ona li kama ala tan jan wan wan; ona li kama ala tan sona pi jan.

**jo mani.** mani anu ijo awen pi jan pali jo li lon jo la, o toki e ni lon lipu nasin pi ilo: jan seme li jo e ona? kepeken nasin seme? ilo li pini pali la, jan pali jo li kama jo sin kepeken nasin seme? o nimi e jan ale pi jo mani lon linja — e jan pali ale pi jan ante — ilo li pilin ala e mani la kin. mani awen (escrow) li tenpo wan taso pi ni: ijo pi jan pali jo li ken moli lon moli pi jan pi ilo suli; tan ni la, lukin pi wile ona li kama tan toki en tan ken weka: jo mani pi toki ala li pakala pali tan §10.6, li tan pi wile weka tan §12.3.

**jan lukin.** o awen e lipu pi tawa nasin; jan lukin awen tu li lukin e ona. ona li kama tan esun pi kipisi ilo pi kulupu, kepeken nasin alasa ken; nanpa open pi alasa li tan lipu suli pi jan ale: jan ale li ken lukin e alasa tan §4.1(a). jan pi ilo suli li pana e kipisi ilo ona tan §10.3 la, jan lukin ona li kama tan esun kin; jan li ken ala lukin e ijo sama ona. o pana e mani tawa esun tawa pali lukin, kepeken nanpa mani awen pi lipu nasin pi ilo; esun li pana e jan lukin e mani tawa ona; jan pi ilo suli li pana ala e mani tawa jan lukin wan. tomo lukin ale li awen lon sike tu; tomo tu li lon tenpo ante: wan li kama sin lon sike ale; kama sin li lon lon tenpo pi suno sama pi mun tu wan, lon poka pi lipu jan pi §3.9. jan lukin pi tomo pini li awen jo li awen toki e lipu pi lukin ona. nanpa mani pi lawa utala tan §10.9 li lon lipu nasin kin; ona li kama ala tan utala wan.

**lipu esun.** o awen e lukin pi jan ante lon pali ale pi toki awen.

**ken lawa.** o pana e ilo pi §4.4 lon sinpin pi ilo — jan pali jo li ken kama jan kulupu, li ken wile e kulupu ona li ken ante e ona tan §3.9, li ken open e ijo li ken pana e mu lon tomo toki pi kulupu ona — o pana ala e mu ni, o lawa ala e ona.

**10.3 wile pi kipisi ilo.** o esun e poki e pali nanpa tan esun pi kipisi ilo pi kulupu, anu o pana e poki e pali nanpa sina. jan li kama jo e jan pali jo sama tomo pi kipisi ilo la, ona o toki e ni lon sinpin pi kama: lipu wile pi linja tomo (residential connection) li ken pini e pali tomo pi kama mani; tomo li ken lukin e pali nanpa pi ilo ona.

**10.4 lawa utala li kama jo e pana.** lawa pi utala li lon la, jan pi utala li pana e nanpa lon lawa pi jan lawa ni — jan pi ilo suli, lon insa pi jan pali jo ona; jan lukin pi lawa, lon utala pi ilo mute en lon utala pi jan pali jo en jan pi ilo suli ona. pana li lon lukin sama nanpa pi pini lon nasin ale — lon ala nanpa wan — tawa ilo, tawa jan lukin kin.

**10.5 tenpo utala.** nasin pi kule pi esun en sona li toki ala e tenpo utala ona la, lipu nasin pi ilo li pana e tenpo open.

**10.6 pakala pali.** pakala awen pi pali pi §§10.1–10.3 li ijo pi toki awen nanpa wan — lawa utala, pana nanpa, en weka pona pi esun li lawa e ona. ona li ijo pi jan kulupu lon tenpo ni taso: ona li weka pi wile wan pi §3.2(b)–(e), anu lipu ni li nimi e pakala ni sama tan pi wile weka tan §12.3.

**10.7 lawa insa ala.** kulupu NTARI li lukin ala, li pona ala, li ken ala sin e lawa mani pi jan pi ilo suli. weka pi jan pali jo li pona — lon mani awen, en lon ilo pi nasin tu pi ni: jan pali jo ale li wile e nasin pi kama ona tan §11.5 — la, lukin li tan nasin sama: lipu li kama lon lipu suli pi jan ale lon tenpo pali; sona jan li kama tan lawa pi lon lukin; nanpa sewi en nanpa pakala li lon lukin; jan pali jo li ken weka. weka li ike — ilo pi mani pi kulupu taso, pi ni: nanpa pona pi jan pali jo li kama ala mani ma, li kama ala jo sin, taso nanpa ike li wile pana — la, §2.2 li wile e mu; Article XI li pana e ona: jan pali jo pi ilo ni li wile e ante tan §11.1(d). mu li ona taso; kulupu insa ala pi kulupu NTARI li kama lon tomo ona; kipisi ni li lupa ala tan §7.6.

**10.8 toki copyleft.** o pali sama GNU Affero General Public License en nasin copyleft ona.

**10.9 jan seme li lawa e utala.** pakala ken pi toki awen lon insa pi jan pali jo tu pi ilo sama la, jan pi ilo suli pi ilo ni li lawa e ona — tan §10.2. utala pi ilo tu la, jan lukin pi esun ni li lawa, lon kipisi pi jan lukin; jan pi ilo suli tu li lawa ala: ala wan li insa lon jan pali jo ona en jan pali jo pi ilo ante. utala pi jan pali jo en jan pi ilo suli pi ilo ona — tan esun, tan jo mani, tan nanpa wan pi mani, tan ante tenpo tan Article XI, anu tan pali pi kipisi ni — la, jan lukin pi ilo ni li lawa; jan pi ilo suli li lawa ala: ona li insa ala lon ijo ona sama. jan lukin pi lawa li kama jo e pana lon lawa ona tan §10.4, sama jan pi ilo suli; lipu pi nasin pi ike en lawa li pana e nasin. jan lukin tu li wile e tu ante la, jan lukin sin wan li kama: nasin alasa pi §10.2 li pana e ona tawa utala ni taso; esun li kama jo e mani lon tenpo utala taso. kulupu lawa li jan tu wan tawa utala pi jan pali jo en jan pi ilo suli ona — jan lukin awen tu pi ilo, en tomo alasa; li jan luka tawa utala pi ilo tu — jan lukin awen tu pi ilo ale, en tomo alasa.

## Article XI — esun pi mani pi kulupu

**11.1 lupa.** ilo esun li open kepeken mani awen (escrow) sama nasin open pi esun — ijo awen li lon; nanpa ike li lon ala; mani jan li pana ala. lukin wan pi wile pi mani awen li ni: jan pi ilo suli li jo pona e mani; §10.2 li wile e toki pi jo ni.

ilo li kama ilo pi nasin tu (hybrid) pi mani pi kulupu lon tenpo ni taso: wile (a) tawa (c) ale li lon. ona li kama ilo pi mani pi kulupu taso (full) lon tenpo ni taso: wile (a) tawa (d) ale li lon:

(a) jan pi ilo suli li pali e ken pi lawa e tenpo kama ona, sama lawa pi ma ona;

(b) kulupu pi jan pali jo li kama sona tan §11.3;

(c) lipu pana pi wile §11.2 li lon; li

(d) jan pali jo pi ilo ni li wile e ante: lon mu pi jo e toki sona tan §11.3, pi tenpo esun wan anu mute la, mu pana pi tu pi mute tu wan (2/3) li wile e ante, LI nanpa pi mu pana li suli anu sama tawa wan pi mute tu wan (1/3) pi jan pali jo ale pi ilo pi jo e esun pini lon lipu suli pi jan ale lon tenpo suno 90 pini. jan pali jo ale pi ilo — jan ale pi jo e esun pini pi ilo lon lipu suli pi jan ale; nanpa li tan jan, li tan ala nanpa ilo — li jo e mu wan, lon sinpin pi ilo kepeken ilo pi §4.4; jan pi ilo suli li ken ala pana e ona, li ken ala lawa e ona, li ken ala awen e ona, li ken ala wile e ijo tan ona, tan §4.5. mu, lipu jan ona, en pini ona li kama lon lipu pi kulupu lawa, lon poka pi lipu pana pi §11.2.

ilo pi nasin tu li wile ala e mu ni, tan ni: jan pali jo ale li wile e nasin pi kama ona tan §11.5; weka pi jan ala li kama ike lon ala wile ona. ilo li tawa tan nasin tu tawa mani pi kulupu taso la, ona li pali sin e (d). wile ale pi kama li lon ala la, ante li ken ala kama — ijo ante ale li lon pini la kin. wile ale li wile pi kipisi ni tawa §11.4.

**11.2 lipu pana li pali.** pana pi lipu ken pi ma — lipu pi ken pana e pali pi mani pi kulupu — tawa lipu pi kulupu lawa li pali lawa sama; ma li wile ala e lipu la, pana pi toki "ma li wile ala" li pali sama. pana ni li pana e ken tawa esun pi mani pi kulupu lon jan pi ilo linja ale. toki "ma li wile ala" li toki lon lipu; jan li ken utala e ona tan §11.4, sama wile ale pi lupa.

**11.3 toki sona.** jan pi ilo suli li toki tawa jan pali jo ona e tawa kulupu pona, tawa tu ni lon tenpo suno 30 anu mute lon sinpin pi tenpo ante. tawa ante tawa mani pi kulupu taso la, toki li toki e tenpo suno open en tenpo suno pini pi mu pi §11.1(d); ante li kama lon tenpo suno 30 anu mute lon monsi pi toki, li kama ala lon sinpin pi pini mu.

**11.4 utala pi wile ma.** jan kulupu ale, anu jan pali jo ale pi ilo ni, li ken utala e ni: "wile pi §11.1 li lon ala lon!" — kepeken nasin pi Article XII.

**11.5 nasin tu.** lon ilo pi nasin tu la, mani awen en mani pi kulupu li pali lon ilo sama; jan pali jo ale li wile e nasin pi kama ona. pali sama wile pi Article XI en toki sona pi §11.3 li ken pi pali pi nasin tu. nasin tu li tenpo nasin pi ilo mute lon monsi pi mani awen.

**11.6 kama sin.** jan pali jo pi ilo li ken tawa e ilo pi mani pi kulupu taso tawa nasin tu, lon tenpo ale: nanpa suli pi mu pana li wile, lon mu pi tenpo esun wan anu mute, pi jo e toki sona tan §11.3. jan pi ilo suli li pana e ni lon lipu lon ante ale: ijo pi jan li tawa seme tawa nasin tu? ona li pali e ni lon kama sin.

## Article XII — ike, weka, kama sin, en toki sin

**12.1 kipisi lawa taso li ken pali e ni.** jan lawa nanpa tu li awen e lipu lawa ni lon kulupu ilo ale — kepeken nasin pi sinpin pi kipisi lawa, kepeken lipu pi lukin suli lon lukin ale, kepeken wawa kama ni.

**12.2 lukin suli.** lukin suli (audit) li ni: lipu lawa li kama lukin lon lipu pi jan pi ilo linja, pi jan lukin, pi jan pali jo, lon ilo pali kin. jan lawa nanpa tu li pana e lipu pi lukin suli ale tawa jan ale, kepeken nasin §2.4. kulupu pi lawa en kulupu poka pi ijo ni li ken wile e lukin pi ilo kiwen (hardware), lon wile sama.

**12.3 weka en pini lon ilo wan.**

(a) **weka.** weka (expulsion) li pini pi mu pi jan kulupu lon kulupu NTARI, lon tenpo esun 10 anu lili. jan pi lawa utala li pana e wile weka tawa tomo toki pi kulupu lawa; jan pi kulupu ni li wile e ona e tenpo ona, kepeken nanpa suli pi mu pana tan §6.5. weka li kama lon lipu pi kulupu lawa kepeken toki sin; jan weka li ken toki sin e ona tan §12.5. tenpo li pini la, kama sin pi jan li kama lon §12.5(b) lon ala lipu wile; weka pi lipu wile li awen e pini lon tenpo esun 10 sin anu lili.

(b) **pini lon ilo wan.** pini lon ilo wan (bar) li pini pi ken pali pi jan lon ilo wan taso. jan pi ilo suli li ken pini e jan pali jo lon pali jo pi ilo ona lon tenpo ni taso: pana −1 tu wan (3) anu mute pi lawa pini pi jan ni li lon, li lon poka sama sona, li tawa tomo toki pi kulupu lawa kepeken nasin tawa sona en nanpa tan §2.4; ilo pi ilo esun li weka e pini pi jo ala e ona.

**12.4 weka en pini li kama tawa seme.** weka li pini ala e jan lon ilo ale; pini li kama tawa ilo wan taso pi pali e ona. tu li weka ala e tenpo pini: lipu pi jan weka anu jan pini li awen, kepeken toki sin; ona li awen tan ni: jan 6 li jo e ona. tu li kama ala tawa awen pi nasin: ijo en tenpo pini pi jan kulupu li awen lon weka pi sinpin ale; lipu pi kulupu li awen lon weka pi jan pi ilo suli ale, pi jan pi ilo linja ale.

**12.5 nasin pi toki sin en kama sin.** jan kulupu ale, en jan ale pi jo e ken §4.1 anu §11.4, li ken pana e ni: toki sin pi pini pi ken esun; toki sin pi weka; utala tan §11.4; lipu wile pi kama sin lon monsi pi pini anu weka; lipu wile pi kama sin pi jan pi ilo suli pi weka anu pini. nasin pi ale ni li ni:

(a) **pana.** lipu wile li tawa jan lawa nanpa tu. ona li jo ala e nimi jan; jan en esun li lon lipu kepeken nasin tawa sona en nanpa.

(b) **tenpo pana nanpa.** jan lawa nanpa wan li pana e lipu wile lon tomo toki pi kulupu pona, lon tenpo pana pi tenpo esun wan; ona li awen e pana nanpa tawa alasa sona pi §8.2: toki awen li ante e seme lon jan kepeken? toki sin pi weka li kama wile lon tenpo suno 7 anu lili lon monsi pi pana ona.

(c) **pana nanpa.** jan ale pi kulupu li ken pana e nanpa wan pi toki awen lon ike ni, lon nasin nanpa pi §9.9, lon kule lawa pi §9.10(d). −1 li jo e toki tan pi wile §9.10(b), pi nimi 500 anu lili.

(d) **wile.** pana li lon la, pana li wile e lipu wile: nanpa mute (mode) pi pana li −1 la, lipu wile li weka; kama sin li weka. ante la, ona li kama. pana ala li lon la taso, jan wan pi kulupu li wile e lipu wile, lon lipu pi pali ni. lipu wile ala li kama tan ilo taso.

(e) **pana sin.** lipu wile li weka la, jan li ken pana sin lon monsi pi tenpo esun 10.

(f) **nasin sama tawa jan pi ilo suli.** jan pi ilo suli pi weka anu pini li kama sin kepeken nasin sama ni; lipu wile li kama la, sitelen kama pi §3.3 li kama kepeken nasin pali lili ona.

## Article XIII — kulupu NTARI li lawa e kulupu NTARI sama

**13.1 ilo wan ala.** kulupu NTARI li ken ala jo e tomo wan, e poki wan, e nanpa len wan, e jan esun wan pi ni: weka ona li ken pini e kulupu ilo. ijo ale pi jo pi kulupu NTARI o ken kama ante lon ala sona pi kulupu ilo.

**13.2 lipu li awen lon weka pi jan awen.** lipu suli en ilo li jo e nasin pana pi ni: ona li awen lon weka pi kulupu NTARI. lipu suli pi jan ale, lipu pi jan esun ale, en lipu lawa pi ken tawa li awen sama. pali ala pi kulupu pi jan lawa anu pi jan kulupu li ken pakala e pana ken pi tenpo pini, e lipu pi tenpo pini.

**13.3 jan awen kama.** kulupu NTARI li pini la, jan kulupu li wile e jan awen sin tawa lipu suli, tawa ilo lukin, tawa lipu pi kulupu lawa — lon sinpin pi pini ale. ijo mani li tawa sama wile pi lipu open pi kulupu en pi lawa pi §1.2. pini pi kulupu NTARI li ijo pi kipisi lawa, li ijo ala pi kulupu ilo: ilo esun ala, mani ala, lipu ala, esun ala li wile e lon pi kulupu NTARI — kipisi ilo taso li wile.

---

## Article XIV — lawa lili pi kulupu

**14.1 sike mani.** sike mani li sike pi tenpo suno ale (calendar year).

**14.2 lipu mani en lipu ale.** kulupu NTARI li awen e lipu ale pi wile pi KRS Chapter 273 en pi nasin 501(c)(3), lon poka pi lipu pi kulupu lawa pi §9.5. jan kulupu li ken lukin e lipu pi kulupu NTARI kepeken toki pi tenpo pona.

**14.3 wile tu.** jan lawa ale, jan pali lawa ale, en jan wan ale li jo e ijo mani lon ijo kulupu la, ona o toki e ni lon sinpin pi pali ona, o pana ala e mu lon tenpo pi wile tu. kulupu pi jan lawa li jo e lipu nasin pi wile tu, sama wile pi nasin 501(c)(3) ona; toki ni li kama lipu tan §2.5.

**14.4 mani pali.** jan lawa li kama jo e mani ala tan pali lawa ona. kulupu NTARI li ken pana e mani pona tawa jan pali en jan esun tawa pali pana ona, kepeken lawa pi §1.2.

**14.5 awen pi jan lawa.** kulupu NTARI li awen e jan lawa ona, e jan pali lawa ona, e jan wan ona, tan ike mani — lon suli pi ken pi KRS Chapter 273 en pi nasin 501(c)(3) ona. ona li ken esun e lipu awen (insurance) tawa ni.

**14.6 sama tawa jan ale.** sitelen kama, ken pi jan pali jo, en nasin ale pi lipu ni li pali sama tawa jan ale; kule jan li lawa ala e ona. nasin wan pi §3.1 en wile pi §3.2 li lukin taso pi kama jan kulupu.

**14.7 lipu lili.** lipu lawa ni li awen lili, li awen pona lukin — sama wile pi lipu suli tawa ilo. toki sin li kama lon tenpo ni taso: ona li nimi e wawa, e pali wile, anu e lukin pi lon ala lipu ante pi kulupu lipu. lipu suli, ilo lukin, anu lipu pi nasin pi ike en lawa li lawa e ijo la, lipu ni li toki e nimi ona, li toki sin ala e toki ona. toki pi kama jo e lon tu li weka kepeken ante tan Article XV.

---

## Article XV — ante

**15.1 jan kulupu taso.** lipu lawa ni li kama ante tan nanpa suli pi mu pi jan wan mute taso; kulupu ale li wile lon insa tan §6.5; jan ale pi kulupu ni — jan pali jo kin — li jo e mu; mu li jo e toki sona tan §6.4 pi jo e toki ale pi ante.

**15.2 anpa.** ante li ken ala pakala e nasin 12, li ken ala lawa e lipu suli, li ken ala weka e lukin pi wile pi §2.2 tawa wawa ale.

**15.3 awen open.** ante kama li awen open tan §2.3, sama wile ale.

---

## Article XVI — tenpo ante

**16.1 tenpo open.** lon tenpo ni la, kulupu pi jan open li lawa e kulupu NTARI: jan lawa tu wan anu mute, luka anu lili. ona li pali e kulupu ilo JFA nanpa wan. jan kulupu ante pi sitelen kama tan §3.3 li kama jan wan pi kulupu la taso, lawa li ante; lon sinpin pi ni la, kulupu pi jan open li kulupu pi jan lawa, li jo e wawa pi jan kulupu. pali ale pi wawa ni li kama lon lipu pi kulupu lawa sama pali pi tenpo open. pali ni li awen lon, taso kulupu ale li ken lukin sin e ona tan §2.3, sama wile ale.

**16.2 pini pi tenpo open.** kulupu pi jan open li pini lon tenpo ni: kulupu ale li wile e jan wan ona. jan wan pi kama lon sinpin pi nanpa ni li lawa lon poka pi kulupu pi jan open. jan wan pi ilo — jan anu kulupu esun — li ken ala wile e ona sama tawa tomo ni. kulupu li wile e ni:

- **ilo pini** — kipisi ale (nasin, lawa linja, en sinpin) li jo e ilo pini ale
- **lukin pini** — kipisi ale li kama lukin lon nasin utala
- **lipu pana** — kipisi ale li lon poki lipu lawa, sama lipu nasin insa

**16.3 tomo toki pi lawa en pi toki awen lon tenpo open.** kulupu pi lawa li jo e lipu pi lawa e kulupu ante; kipisi nasin ona li kulupu NTARI sama. tan ni la, wile pi §16.2 li lawa e kipisi pi lawa linja en pi sinpin ona taso. ona li ken wile e jan wan ona lon tenpo ni: kipisi tu ni li pona tawa wile ni, LI ijo tu sin li lon — jan ilo pi sitelen kama li lawa e ilo lawa, LI ilo pi §4.4 li lon lon ilo pi kulupu wan anu mute: ni la jan pali jo li ken kama tan §3.8, li ken pana e mu. kulupu pi lawa li jo e jan ilo wan taso la, wile pi §16.2 pi "wile ala e sama" li kama pona tan mu pi jan pali jo: jan ilo ni li pana ala e mu ni, li lawa ala e ona (§4.5). kulupu pi lawa pi jo e jan ilo wan en jan pali jo ala li wile e jan ala. kulupu pi jan open li sitelen e ni lon lipu pi kulupu lawa, sama pali pi tenpo open: tenpo open pi tomo toki, en lipu jan pi tenpo ni.

kulupu pi toki awen li sama: ona li ken wile e jan wan ona lon tenpo ni: kipisi ona li pona tawa wile pi §16.2, LI ilo pi §4.4 li lon lon ilo pi kulupu wan anu mute, LI jan pali jo wan anu mute li wile e kulupu ni tan §3.9. wile pi §16.2 pi "wile ala e sama" li lawa e ona sama kulupu pi lawa: kulupu pi toki awen pi jo e jan ilo wan en jan pali jo ala li wile e jan ala. kulupu pi jan open li sitelen e tenpo open en lipu jan pi tomo toki ale, sama sewi.
