# Governance Venue Policy

**P1-005 · Version 1.0 (draft) · pending adoption**

Board policy under bylaws §5.6. It takes effect when adopted by the founder board as a bootstrap act under §16.1 and recorded in the governance registry under §9.5. Changing venues is a policy act and never requires amendment of the bylaws (§5.6).

**1. Venue in force.** The Institute's governance venue is Slack, provided by the Salesforce Corporation.

**2. Interim status.** Slack is a proprietary venue. It is used only as a named interim under §5.6.

**3. Committed exit.** Once all subordinate layers of the stack — Substrate, Record, Covenant, and Economy & Information — are built, tested, and operationally maintained, the Governance layer follows: the Institute moves its coordination to the JFA governance platform and ends its use of Slack as a venue.
