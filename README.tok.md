## lipu lawa ni li pali e seme

nasin JFA li pana e ma wan taso tawa kulupu NTARI: ona li lon nasin insa
(protocol) pi kipisi lawa (Governance). kulupu NTARI li kulupu pi wile mani ala
(nonprofit, 501(c)(3)). ona li awen pona e ilo pi jan ale (copyleft). lipu lawa ni li lawa
e kulupu ni. kipisi §1.4 li toki e ni: kulupu li lon tan ni: ona o jo e lipu
lawa JFA e ilo ona pi lukin sama (ilo li ken pali e ona), o awen e ona, o ante e
ona; ona o awen pona e ilo JFA sama ijo pi jan ale, lon nasin ni (copyleft); ona o
alasa sona e nasin pi linja ilo, e ilo pi toki kulupu, e nasin mani pi insa
kulupu; ona o pana e sona ni tawa jan ale.

## lipu ante pi sona wan

kipisi §1.5 li pana e sona pi lipu tu wan. lipu ni li lon ala poki ni:

| nimi lon lipu lawa | ona li lon seme |
|---|---|
| "lipu lawa JFA" ("the official document") | [`janus-facing-architecture.md`](https://github.com/NTARI-RAND/Janus/blob/main/janus-facing-architecture.md) lon NTARI-RAND/Janus |
| "linja 12" ("the twelve lines") | kipisi pi lipu lawa JFA. nimi ona li *The Lines That Cannot Be Crossed* |
| "nasin pali pi toki utala" ("the dispute-mechanics design") | `jfa-dispute-mechanics.md`, lon poki lipu NTARI; tenpo ni la ona li kama ala lon poki pi jan ale |

sitelen pi lipu lawa ni li lon ni sama lipu wile, lon ijo lili ale
(byte-for-byte). linja tu ona (`../JFA/janus-facing-architecture.md` en
`../JFA/jfa-dispute-mechanics.md`) li pali lon poki lipu NTARI taso, li pali ala
lon poki ni. jan lukin li lon ni la, poki nanpa sewi li pana e nasin tawa lipu
lon tenpo ale. jan li ante ala e sitelen tawa pona pi linja ni. tan ni: kulupu li wile e lipu
lawa la, ona li ante e ona kepeken kipisi suli XV, li pona lili e ona lon tenpo
ala.
