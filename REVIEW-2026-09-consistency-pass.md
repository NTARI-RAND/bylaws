### Reading section 10

The lenses overlap, so several findings above appear two or three times. Deduplicated, the surviving findings are these.

**Fixed after verification (bylaws `1d7cef5`).** §10.2 Governance Access named only the Governance channel; §3.9 said the election places "the vote and nothing else" although §3.8 has it place candidacy too; the appendix candidacy row omitted §8.2. These account for all eight "carried, with a defect" verdicts above.

**Created by the election, needing a decision.**

- **Lapse and re-recognition get around the yearly change.** §3.8 lets a member lapse "when the member so elects", and §3.6 readmits by the ordinary recognition path, at which §3.9 lets the member elect a federation afresh. So a member can change federation at any time of year by lapsing and rejoining. Found by three lenses independently.
- **Ballots open across the equinox.** The change window is three to four days and the default ballot runs a week, so ballots straddle the equinox every year. Nothing says which roll a ballot uses, and a member who moves mid-ballot could vote in both federations on the same Institute-wide matter. A one-sentence fix would fix the roll at the moment notice is given.
- **Bootstrap cannot end without a Covenant prosumer.** §16.2 ends bootstrap when every federation has elected, and §16.3 makes the Covenant federation eligible only once at least one prosumer member has chosen it. If none does, bootstrap never ends.
- **A lone prosumer can elect themselves.** §16.2's rule against self-election names "a single operator/orchestrator". A Covenant federation of one prosumer member can elect that member President, who then carries one of five delegate votes.
- **The §6.5 operating-member floor now binds one federation of two.** A Governance ballot is void without an operating member's vote; a Covenant ballot, including the President's election, is valid on one prosumer's vote. This is item 11 of the 2026-09-22 amendment, still undecided.
- **Which channel hears an expelled prosumer's appeal.** §12.5(b) sends petitions to "the concerned federation channel", which is undefined. For a Covenant-federation member expelled by the Governance federation there are now two candidates. One lens found this resolved by other text; another did not.
- **Open question 8** (sybil resistance) still prices a manufactured roll as reaching one Governance delegate. With two prosumer federations it can reach two of five.

**Official document against v1.1, genuine and not closed by PR #16.**

- **Prosumer against its own operator.** PR #15's Covenant paragraph sends these disputes to the witness layer; v1.1 §10.9 has no such case. Item 16 of the 2026-09-22 amendment, still undecided.
- **Readmission after expulsion.** The official document says expulsion is "decided by its members' vote". v1.1 §12.3(a) continues the suspension through §12.5, whose step (d) has the federation's delegate decide whenever the ratings' mode is not −1. Continuation is therefore decided by one person, not by vote.
- **Substrate quarrels.** The official document says quarrels at the Substrate layer "are not adjudicated". v1.1 §10.6 makes persistent failure of every Article X duty, the orchestrator's included, "a covenant matter first — adjudicated".
- **§9.19's last sentence.** The official document now says the chain "is stored by the substrate rather than by the operator". §9.19 leaves commitments of exchanges not carried by an orchestrator to the committing member under §10.3, which permits self-provided storage, so for those commitments the operator may be the store. This is the drafting choice flagged for review in §4.
- **Witness independence** is undefined while witnesses are paid by the operator they may judge. Item 13 of the 2026-09-22 amendment, still undecided.

Pre-existing and already recorded: §6.5 ¶3 against §12.5(d) (N-1), and §3.3's "challengeable through Article XII", which no §12.5 petition type covers.
